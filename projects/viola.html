VIOLA

A safety app from early-stage UX flows to a high-fidelity mobile interface. A sensitive service made clear, fast and reassuring.

Client
Confidential
Role
Lead Product Designer
Platform
iOS · Android
Duration
3 years · 2021—2023

Status
Live in production

01 — Context

The problem

Women facing domestic violence lack reliable tools to ask for help without being noticed. Existing apps were slow, visually aggressive, and required too many steps in moments where seconds count.

VIOLA started as a pilot project with an anti-violence association: an app that, in fewer than three taps, activates a network of trusted contacts and notifies the authorities with a precise location.

02 — Approach

Three design principles

  1. Invisible until needed. The app must go unnoticed on the phone. Neutral icon, generic name, screens that don't look like an SOS.
  2. Three taps at most. Every critical flow — alert, call, silent message — must be completable in three interactions, even in the dark.
  3. Reassuring, not alarming. Warm palette, soft typography, gentle microcopy. The design doesn't increase anxiety — it reduces it.

03 — Process

From flow to interface

The work started with 14 interviews with operators from anti-violence centres and 6 with potential users (under NDA and psychological supervision). They produced 3 primary user flows, 8 system states, and a 42-component design system.

3× faster activation time vs. baseline
42 components in the design system
98% task success rate in usability testing

04 — Outcome

What changed

VIOLA was adopted in 7 Italian cities during the pilot programme. The design system is now used as the foundation for other products across the organisation, ensuring continuity and development speed. The project continues with new iterations focused on accessibility and localisation.
